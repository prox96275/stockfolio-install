# Disclaimer: not financial or tax advice

Stockfolio is a **record-keeping and reporting tool**. It helps you track investments and prepare figures for your
tax return or your tax agent.

- **Not advice.** Nothing in Stockfolio is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. It doesn't consider your
  personal circumstances, and it doesn't recommend buying, selling or holding anything. Charts such as allocation,
  performance and diversification are information only.
- **Check the figures.** Tax results depend on what you enter and on rules Stockfolio applies in general ways
  (for example parcel matching, the CGT discount, franking credits and the CGT changes from 1 July 2027). Check the
  reports against your broker and share registry statements, and against ATO guidance. Have a registered tax agent
  review them if you're unsure.
- **You lodge your return.** You or your tax agent remain responsible for what's lodged with the ATO.
- **Not covered automatically:** takeovers and demergers, options, CGT rollovers, small-business concessions and the
  old indexation method for assets bought before 21 September 1999. You need to enter these yourself, following
  the issuer's or your tax agent's guidance.
- **Prices can be wrong or late.** Market prices, dividends and exchange rates come from third-party sources and may
  be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Don't use Stockfolio for trading decisions.
- **Tax law changes.** Stockfolio follows the law as we understand it when each version is released. Rules and ATO
  practice can change, and an older version won't know about later changes.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law aren't affected. See [LICENCE-AGREEMENT.md](LICENCE-AGREEMENT.md).
